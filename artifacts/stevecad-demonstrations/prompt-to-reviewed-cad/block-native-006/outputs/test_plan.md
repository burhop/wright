# Rectangular Four-Hole Block — Compact Test Plan

## Scope and verdict rules

Inspect the single component designated `Block_60x40x12` (upstream design choice A01) in its part-local frame, which equals world coordinates by identity transform. Units are millimetres. The origin is the lower-left corner of the bottom face; +X, +Y, and +Z correspond to length, width, and thickness.

Use evaluated CAD/B-rep geometry and measurement results, not source code or construction intent. Missing required evidence is **UNVERIFIED**; a measured violation is **FAIL**. No later review may relax these limits. CAD inspection covers geometry and native captures only. Rendering and final-document checks remain deferred until those outputs exist and are not CAD-inspection failures.

## Interface, clearance, and motion scope

The only geometric interfaces are the external planar faces and four bore cylinders. Verify their topology under R03 and R07. No mating interface, assembly clearance, or motion is specified; therefore clearance and motion tests are **N/A**, not passes. No hardware, supports, or fasteners may be introduced.
