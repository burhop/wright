# Digital inspection brief

This is a proposed inspection plan for the supplied requirements, not evidence that a plate has been manufactured or measured.

| Feature | Supplied requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Length | 120 mm |
| Width | 80 mm |
| Thickness | 6 mm |
| Mounting holes | Four holes, each 8 mm in diameter |

Check the intended length, width and thickness; confirm four holes and record each hole's diameter. Once their requirements are defined, also check hole-center locations and their relationships to the specified datums. Keep the measured observations and acceptance criteria with each check.

Hole-center coordinates, spacing/pattern, datums, dimensional and positional tolerances, hole depth/through status and material are unspecified. Do not infer these from the nominal dimensions or invent a passing criterion. No additional values have been assumed.

No physical inspection, CAD measurement or tool execution occurred in this task. Manufacturing release cannot be claimed without defined acceptance criteria, actual measurement evidence and the required engineering review.
