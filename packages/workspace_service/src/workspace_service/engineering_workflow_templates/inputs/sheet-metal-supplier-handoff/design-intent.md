# Demo bracket intent

Create a one-piece bent prototype bracket from the accompanying concept image. Preserve four mounting holes and one bend. Treat every missing dimension, load, tolerance, material, thickness, bend radius, finish, and supplier option as an explicit assumption or unresolved requirement. Do not infer approval from the image.

The workflow must retain indexed CAD attempts, stop after at most two automatic corrective revisions, and produce native PSM, folded STEP, and a genuine developed DXF. Supplier interaction stops at a verified preview and user-controlled cart or quote handoff. Wright must not order, pay, accept production-releasing credit, or contact supplier support.
