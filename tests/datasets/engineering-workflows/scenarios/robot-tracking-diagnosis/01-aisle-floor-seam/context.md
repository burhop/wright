# Aisle seam investigation
Original fictional commissioning logs for an unloaded 32 kg differential-drive cart. Warehouse test lane is 8 m long and 1.5 m wide; no people were present in this synthetic run. Data covers 0..30 s at 0.5 s nominal spacing. Planned speed is 0.22 m/s along map +x from origin. All frames are already map-aligned and all clocks share the same elapsed-time origin. External tracker sample repeatability is treated as unknown, so do not attach a calibrated confidence interval. The operator event at 15.0 s labels a taped seam crossing. Wheel radius 0.075 m and track width 0.42 m are supplied descriptive values, not measured calibration. Company policy requires one inexpensive follow-up experiment and no remote controller changes.
## Data contract and reproducibility
All logs are original synthetic numbers, supplied in the form a customer can export from a recorder and upload. Time values are elapsed seconds, positions metres, yaw radians, velocity m/s or rad/s as labeled. They are not records of a real robot and do not establish a real failure cause. The operator annotation is a supplied observation to correlate, not a diagnosis.
The current workflow starts with ROS 2 bag inspection. These portable CSV uploads require a traceable CSV-to-ROS-2-bag preparation step before that adapter can consume them. Use the documented topic/message mapping in bag-conversion.md, retain original CSVs, and preserve timing/frame offsets. Do not rename CSV files to .db3 or .mcap or claim they are a bag. If conversion or ROS message serialization is unavailable, report that input-adapter blocker explicitly.
For analysis, state frame alignment, interpolation and excluded intervals. Report duration/sample count, lateral and position-error RMSE, and velocity disagreement. Correlate the one annotated event within one sample. Distinguish evidence from hypotheses; command/odometry disagreement alone is not proof of wheel slip. Do not command a physical robot, change its controller or connect to a production ROS graph.
## Requested outputs
Write timeline.csv, trajectory-overlay.svg or PNG, tracking-metrics.csv or JSON, and diagnosis.md or PDF with links to supporting time ranges. The initial harness checks files exist only; the content-validity metric must remain zero.
