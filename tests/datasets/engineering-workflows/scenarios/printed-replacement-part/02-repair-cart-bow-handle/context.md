# Drawer-pull design context

## Work setting
Loopside has a rolling small-parts drawer used to hold valve caps and service labels. The handle is a convenience accessory; it is not used to lift the cart, restrain a load, or prevent access to moving equipment. All business and operating figures are fictional.

## Coordinate system and supplied measurements
Use millimetres. X follows the drawer width, Y runs vertically on its face, and Z points outward. Mounting-plane Z is zero. Bolt centers are (0,0) and (96,0); use their 96.0 mm separation as the image scale reference. Each pad is 18 mm in X by 24 mm in Y, with 4.5 mm through-hole clearance for the supplied M4 bolt. Pad thickness is 5 mm. Pads may extend outward from the center line but cannot overlap a drawer label below Y=-18 mm.

The bow's outer envelope is at most 118 mm wide, 28 mm high, and 34 mm proud of the drawer. Provide at least 25 mm finger clearance between the back of the central grip and the drawer over a central 65 mm span. Nominal grip section is an oval 12 mm high by 9 mm thick. Internal blends should be at least 3 mm where space permits; all touched edges should have at least 1 mm rounding. Provide a shallow 1.5 mm thumb recess along the center 40 mm. The sketch is intent; do not interpret stroke thickness as material.

## Process and company policy
Target P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle, PETG, 0.20 mm layer, five walls, 40% infill. Existing hardware is two M4 through bolts and external washers; bolt lengths are selected by the human after the measured prototype. The proposed 40 N drawer-opening load is a design intent only; no fatigue or strength claim is requested. Ambient use is 10 to 40 degrees C with wipe cleaning; no food contact or dishwasher use.

The planner should compare a pads-down orientation with a side orientation. Preserve a support preview and describe support-contact locations. Export source and repaired meshes and the final 3MF. Review dimensions before slicing. A local integration-test simulator may acknowledge the package with its digest and a simulated destination; do not connect to a real printer. No standards certification is claimed and no physical fit is prevalidated.
