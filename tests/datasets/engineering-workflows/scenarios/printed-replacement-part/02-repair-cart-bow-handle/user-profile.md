# User profile

Jonah Vale is the fictional maintenance lead at the fictional Loopside Bicycle Repair Cooperative. He orders hardware, measures repair fixtures, and prints workshop accessories on a P1S. He wants a handle that looks deliberate on the front of the repair cart and that a gloved technician can grip. He can upload a dimensioned side view but prefers a plain-language design review to CAD parameters.

People, company, repair volumes, and dimensions are synthetic test information.
