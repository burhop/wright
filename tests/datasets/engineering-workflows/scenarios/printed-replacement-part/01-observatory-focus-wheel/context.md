# Focus-wheel replacement brief

## Business context and supplied data
Willowbank runs three evening public sessions weekly. The fixture represents a manually turned wheel removed from a fictional telescope. The supplied dimensions are invented caliper-like inputs for an integration test, not measurements of any commercial telescope.

## Geometry and use
- Envelope: 38.0 mm maximum diameter and 18.0 mm overall height, including a 2 mm raised front hub.
- Six equally spaced shallow scallops; minimum grip diameter 33 mm. Round exposed edges with a nominal 1.0 mm radius.
- D shaft: 6.00 mm diameter; across-flat distance 5.20 mm. Blind socket depth 10.0 mm, concentric to the wheel. Add 0.20 mm radial clearance to the round boundary and 0.20 mm clearance to the flat. These are prototype choices, not verified fits.
- Retain at least 3.0 mm material above the blind socket. No set screw, metal insert, taper, or invented keyway.
- Target hand torque 0.15 N m and ambient use 0 to 35 degrees C; these are intended use assumptions, not validated performance requirements.

## Capabilities and internal policy
A P1S with 0.4 mm nozzle is the target machine. Desired production-prototype material is PETG, 0.20 mm layer height, four perimeters, and 35% infill; the installed matching machine/process/filament profiles must be selected explicitly. A PLA-only local environment may create a clearly named geometry prototype but must not label it PETG. Do not silently substitute profile material.

This part is for an unloaded training mechanism. Do not claim strength, fit, dimensional accuracy, standards compliance, or successful printing merely because files exist. Proposed dimension tolerance is +/-0.25 mm on non-mating surfaces. Record the D-socket design values separately from any later measured artifact values.

## Review and outputs
Review the socket interpretation, orientation, supports if needed, and exact final package. The dataset authorizes local generation and a labeled simulator receipt only. Real printer credentials, transfer, and print start are outside the pack. No special technical standard is asserted. Current tests inspect output-file existence; engineering validation remains pending.
