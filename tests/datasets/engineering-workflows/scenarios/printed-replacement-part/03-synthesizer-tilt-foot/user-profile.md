# User profile

Leila Moss is a fictional independent sound designer who runs the fictional Mossroom Audio Studio. She owns a desktop synthesizer and prints cosmetic repair parts. She can measure mounting pads and upload sketches, but needs help choosing mesh orientation and avoiding supports inside countersinks. She wants an attractive pair of feet rather than a visibly improvised repair.

The artist, studio, instrument, and recorded dimensions are all synthetic. This pack contains no authentic brand designs.
