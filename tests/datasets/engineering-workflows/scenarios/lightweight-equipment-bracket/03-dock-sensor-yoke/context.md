# Dock sensor yoke brief
All organizations, people and requirements are original fictional test data.
## Customer and manufacturing capability
Arcline makes dock-position indicators. Yokes are milled from a single billet in two setups. Minimum tool diameter 8 mm, internal radius 4 mm, wall 4 mm, and final minimum distance from any bolt bore edge to an outside edge 6 mm. Do not propose additive manufacturing or a welded assembly. Quantity 20 for prototype pilot; no production release is requested.
## Baseline geometry
Use x along the base's long direction, y across its depth and z vertically. Base: x=-60..60,y=-30..30,z=0..8. Left cheek: x=-60..-52,y=-30..30,z=8..88. Right cheek: x=52..60,y=-30..30,z=8..88. Crossbar: x=-52..52,y=-30..-22,z=72..88. Base holes have axis z, diameter 6.6 mm, centers (x,y)=(-42,-18),(-42,18),(42,-18),(42,18). Cheek pivot holes have axis x, diameter 8.2 mm, center y=0,z=62, through each cheek. Preserve a pivot-bearing annulus of outside diameter 24 mm and the complete crossbar outside faces. The service access region x=-35..35,y=-15..30,z=15..50 must remain empty.
## Mechanics and design target
Fix cylindrical bearing surfaces of the four base holes for this first comparative model. Distribute a 100 N load in +y over the crossbar face x=-20..20,y=-22,z=74..86. Evaluate displacement at the crossbar load centroid including torsion, and retain the full stress field. Aim for 25% mass reduction using cheek/base pockets while holding datum faces and holes. Mesh both revisions consistently, then refine; explain any impossible target without changing requirements. This static case does not qualify impact or fatigue.
## Material and analysis basis
Use an explicitly homogeneous, isotropic linear-elastic aluminum design material: density 2700 kg/m³, Young's modulus 69 GPa, Poisson ratio 0.33. These are customer-supplied simulation assumptions, not a mill certificate or an assertion of a particular alloy temper. Maximum evaluated von Mises stress: 120 MPa; load-point displacement: 0.50 mm. Apply a 100 N static force distributed across the stated load patch. No fatigue, impact, bolt preload, joint slip or production certification is included.
Model the stated bolt-bearing restraint, document simplifications, and report reaction forces. Do not use a point-force maximum at a sharp corner as a meaningful design stress. State the stress-evaluation region and preserve the unfiltered field. Compare two meshes with less than 5% change in declared response metrics before choosing the lighter design. Preserve baseline and revised geometries and field files.
## Test output request
Export baseline.step, revised.step, at least one CalculiX-compatible .inp deck, displacement/stress fields, mass-comparison.csv, and analysis-report.md or PDF. The test harness initially checks output-file presence only. Engineering validity remains unmeasured and these results are not authorized for production release.
