# Pilot batch-dispenser study
Original synthetic industrial development brief.
## Business objective and allowed scope
Bracken makes laboratory process fixtures, prototype quantities 2..5. This dispenser is a conceptual atmospheric reservoir; no pressure vessel, sterilization, food-safety, potable-water or regulatory certification is claimed or requested. It contains 1.500 kg water, start 20°C, target 90°C, ambient 20°C. Maximum time 400 s. The simulation selects a future prototype power level; it does not operate a heater.
## Declared parameter study
Effective vessel heat capacity 480 J/K; electrical-to-thermal efficiency 0.94. Nominal UA=2.40 W/K, low UA=1.92 W/K and high UA=2.88 W/K are deliberately supplied uncertainty cases, not fitted measurements. Candidate powers 1200,1500,1800 W. Evaluate the nine combinations only if the approved recorded kit supports the requested quantities. Maintain the input mass and temperature target. Requested stop time 700 s and output interval 1 s.
## Mechanical and team context
The packaging team expects a cylindrical inner cavity diameter about 140 mm and working water depth about 97 mm, with a vented lid and a non-modeled dispensing tube. Geometry is conceptual; the simulation basis is the mass and effective heat capacity. Team policy favors the lowest candidate that meets the time target in every declared supported loss case. Document unmet cases instead of adjusting bounds. Compare tighter available integration settings for the controlling case and record the exact output files. Real convection/lid losses and mixing will be tested later and must not be represented as validated by this study.

## Shared physical assumptions
Water remains a well-mixed single liquid phase from 20°C to 90°C; supplied water heat capacity is 4180 J/(kg·K), treated as constant for this design comparison. This is a simplified prototype assumption, not a fluid-property certification. Constant electrical power is requested until first crossing of target temperature, then heating stops; no boil, steam, dispensing or closed-loop overshoot claim is in scope. Effective vessel heat capacity and linear loss coefficient are supplied assumptions for model compatibility review. Distinguish electrical input energy, modeled heat transferred and modeled ambient loss.
## Requested outputs and initial validation
Retain temperature-time.csv with scenario/power/case IDs, power-comparison.csv, energy-summary.csv, temperature-curves.svg or PNG, heater-selection.md or PDF and kit-parameters.json. Include exact kit/version, accepted parameter names, run IDs, stop time and integration settings. Repeat nominal candidates with a tighter available solver timestep/tolerance and keep the comparison evidence. File presence is the initial automated check; engineering correctness is not yet measured and none of these prototype simulations certifies a heater appliance.
