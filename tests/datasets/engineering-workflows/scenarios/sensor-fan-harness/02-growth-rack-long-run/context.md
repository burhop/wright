# Research growth rack wiring brief
Original fictional user/company requirements.
## Operating conditions
One 24 V DC externally current-limited 6 A supply feeds two identical ventilation fans and one humidity transmitter. Each fan takes 1.20 A continuous and 2.00 A at startup; use simultaneous startup of both. Transmitter takes 0.025 A; its output is 0..10 V with separate signal-return conductor. Cable operating ambient: 45°C; humidity can be high but no immersion or claimed enclosure protection rating is supplied. Exact sealed connector selection is a workflow task, not a presumed certification.
## Route and nets
J0 six-pin controller interface: 1 +24V, 2 POWER_GND, 3 FAN_A_TACH, 4 FAN_B_TACH, 5 HUM_SIGNAL, 6 SIGNAL_RETURN. JA/JB each use pin 1 +24V, 2 POWER_GND, 3 TACH. JH uses pin 1 +24V, 2 SIGNAL_RETURN, 3 HUM_SIGNAL. J0→junction B0 trunk is 1200 mm. B0→JA 2200 mm; B0→JB 2800 mm; B0→JH 1800 mm. Supply 24V and power return have separate splices at B0. Signal return runs continuously to J0 pin 6, tied to power return only at the controller star point. Tach signals are open-collector compatible with the customer's controller by assumption; selection must verify it.
## Assembly and procurement needs
Fans unplug independently without opening the trunk. Use abrasion sleeve on the common trunk and clips at 300 mm maximum intervals, with 50 mm minimum separation from irrigation tubing. Required power-contact current rating is at least 5 A at J0/B0 and 3 A at each fan connector, with manufacturer derating checked at 45°C. Signal contacts need at least 0.1 A. Optimize for stock wire sizes, not minimum copper mass. No live growth-rack connection is requested.
## Fabrication capability and policies
In-house technicians can cut/strip stranded copper conductors, use manufacturer-specified hand-crimp tools and apply heat-shrink labels. They cannot validate an unknown connector family from its appearance. Exact terminal, seal, housing and crimp-tool compatibility must be sourced as part of the workflow. Connector pin tables in this upload are customer interface requirements, not a manufacturer datasheet. Do not fabricate rating citations.
Use allowed-wire.csv for initial resistance calculations: resistances at 20°C are assumed design values, temperature coefficient 0.00393/K, and final conductor/insulation ratings require supplier data. Voltage-drop limit is 3% of 24 V = 0.72 V for each loaded branch, including both supply and return shared segments. Show continuous and simultaneous-start cases separately. Include connector/contact resistance only when sourced or as an explicitly stated assumption; do not silently treat an unknown as a measured zero.
Route-length values are installed centerline paths, one-way, before allowance. Add 50 mm service length at each connector termination plus the chosen connector's documented strip/crimp allowance. Do not double-count shared trunks in cut lists. No physical energizing, machine modification or purchasing is requested.
## Outputs
Write harness-plan.svg/PDF/JSON, pin-schedule.csv, cut-list.csv, bom.csv, assembly.md/PDF and voltage-drop.csv/JSON. Label connector views as mating-face or wire-entry; they must not be mirrored silently. Preserve nets through splices. Test automation initially checks expected files exist, not electrical correctness. These inputs do not certify a production harness.
