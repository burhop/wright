# Dual-zone cabinet harness brief
Original synthetic industrial prototype requirements.
## Business and electrical configuration
Northline plans a pilot of five research cabinets. Supply is 24 V DC, externally current-limited to 5 A. Each fan uses 0.80 A continuous and 1.60 A startup. Each 0..10 V pressure transmitter uses 0.04 A. Worst cable ambient 50°C. Two removable six-pin zone connectors ZX/ZY permit independent service. This is instrumentation wiring only; it does not implement a machinery safety function.
## Pin conventions and separation
J0 controller has eight logical pins: 1 +24V, 2 FAN_RETURN, 3 TACH_X, 4 TACH_Y, 5 PRESSURE_X, 6 PRESSURE_Y, 7 SENSOR_RETURN_X, 8 SENSOR_RETURN_Y. Each zone connector pins are 1 +24V, 2 FAN_RETURN, 3 FAN_TACH, 4 PRESSURE_SIGNAL, 5 SENSOR_RETURN, 6 reserve/unconnected. Mark reserve explicitly; it must not be shorted to ground. JFX/JFY fans: pins 1 +24V,2 FAN_RETURN,3 TACH. JSX/JSY pressure sensors: pins 1 +24V,2 SENSOR_RETURN,3 PRESSURE_SIGNAL. Sensor returns only meet fan ground at J0's external controller star point. Use shielded twisted pairs for pressure signal/return, with shield drain bonded at the controller chassis termination only. Shield drain is not a load return and not a protective-earth conductor.
## Route model
J0→B0 common trunk is 800 mm. B0→ZX is 1800 mm; B0→ZY is 2200 mm. ZX→JFX is 1000 mm and ZX→JSX is 350 mm. ZY→JFY is 1200 mm and ZY→JSY is 400 mm. At each zone connector's load-side branch split, +24V divides between fan and sensor. Fan return is never spliced into sensor return. Shared power trunks must carry sum currents; use exact schedule segments rather than treating every route independently. Select family-specific pin numbering and add mating-face views.
Use documented load-side splices where one zone power pin supplies the fan and sensor. Do not place two conductors into one crimp unless the exact terminal manufacturer's instructions permit that combination; otherwise add an appropriate splice and show its location in the harness plan.
## Service and process controls
Zone X labels blue, Zone Y labels orange; connectors may share a family only if keying or physical reach prevents a cross-connection, justified in the assembly document. Sleeve abrasion points, maintain 40 mm minimum bend radius, and keep at least 50 mm from motor cables. Internal minimum power-contact ratings: J0 4 A; ZX/ZY 2 A per loaded power contact at 50°C. Record manufacturer derating and shield termination choice. Include continuity-test instructions but do not claim a performed electrical test.
## Fabrication capability and policies
In-house technicians can cut/strip stranded copper conductors, use manufacturer-specified hand-crimp tools and apply heat-shrink labels. They cannot validate an unknown connector family from its appearance. Exact terminal, seal, housing and crimp-tool compatibility must be sourced as part of the workflow. Connector pin tables in this upload are customer interface requirements, not a manufacturer datasheet. Do not fabricate rating citations.
Use allowed-wire.csv for initial resistance calculations: resistances at 20°C are assumed design values, temperature coefficient 0.00393/K, and final conductor/insulation ratings require supplier data. Voltage-drop limit is 3% of 24 V = 0.72 V for each loaded branch, including both supply and return shared segments. Show continuous and simultaneous-start cases separately. Include connector/contact resistance only when sourced or as an explicitly stated assumption; do not silently treat an unknown as a measured zero.
Route-length values are installed centerline paths, one-way, before allowance. Add 50 mm service length at each connector termination plus the chosen connector's documented strip/crimp allowance. Do not double-count shared trunks in cut lists. No physical energizing, machine modification or purchasing is requested.
