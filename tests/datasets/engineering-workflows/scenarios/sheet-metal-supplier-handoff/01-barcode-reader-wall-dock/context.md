# Barcode-reader dock brief

## Business need
Parcelwood's fictional packing bench uses a 0.42 kg handheld barcode reader. A dock keeps the reader off the labeling area. Intended prototype quantity is four, and the target finish is unpainted deburred aluminum with rounded external corners. This is a storage accessory, not fall protection or an electrical device.

## Geometry and coordinate basis
All dimensions are in mm and describe the folded part unless noted. X is left-to-right, Y extends out from the wall, and Z is vertical. The shelf is 110 mm wide and 75 mm deep. A 60 mm high back flange rises at Y=0; a 12 mm high front lip rises at Y=75. Both flanges face the same upward Z direction. Side edges remain open. Use nominal 1.5 mm sheet thickness and 2.0 mm inside bend radius as prototype requests pending material/tool capability confirmation.

Two vertical wall-mount slots are centered at X=20 and 90, Z=38 on the back flange. Each is 5.5 mm wide and 12 mm overall long with rounded ends. The shelf has a centered U-shaped cable notch open through the front lip and shelf front edge: width 18 mm in X, depth 20 mm back from Y=75, minimum 3 mm notch-root corner radius. Make the front lip a pair of separated flanges if required for a manufacturable cable opening, and document the interpretation before CAD. Round external non-bend corners 3 mm where feasible. Scanner contact footprint is a synthetic 65 by 50 mm rectangle centered on the shelf; provide 6 mm nominal side clearance.

## Load and process assumptions
The reader's weight and a proposed 15 N handling load are internal concept inputs, not structural verification. The human selects wall anchors separately. No assumptions about wall material or anchor strength are permitted. Select an installed material only after reading back its actual Solid Edge name/properties; if the requested stock or radius is unavailable, retain the gap and create an explicit alternative design revision.
