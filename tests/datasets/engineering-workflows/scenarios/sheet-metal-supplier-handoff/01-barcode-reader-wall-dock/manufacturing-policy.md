Policy PW-PROT-03 is a synthetic internal document, not a published standard.

1. A reviewer accepts dimensions, material identity, thickness, and bend interpretation before release to CAD.
2. Preserve each CAD revision and independent check result. Limit automatic correction to the workflow's existing revision bound; unresolved requirements stay visible.
3. Current supplier information must be retrieved rather than inferred from this brief. No CAD pass is supplier approval.
4. Internal review checkpoints may use an explicitly labeled automated test decision. External-action checkpoints may use only the configured local test simulator for these datasets.
5. No real supplier upload, cart mutation, order, payment, production release, or support message is authorized by this pack.
6. Avoid claiming ISO, OSHA, electrical, or structural compliance. None is specified or assessed here.
