# Fictional prototype purchasing and fabrication policy

Internal reference FB-MFG-12 is synthetic company policy, not an industry standard.

- Use metric drawings and explicit folded versus developed dimensions.
- Material substitutions, radius changes, and manufacturing-driven geometry changes require a recorded design decision before the next CAD revision.
- Prefer one laser-cut, bent part. Do not add welding, fastener insertion, or separate brackets without documenting why.
- Check the exact exported bytes after each geometry revision; invalidate earlier preview associations.
- For integration datasets, local review gates may auto-approve under the test policy. Supplier actions use a labeled local simulator only. Real supplier file transmission, purchase, and payment need separate exact authority.
- Supplier warnings and unknown coating/bend details remain open issues in the handoff. Never invent acceptance, prices, dates, or certification.
