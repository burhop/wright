# Fictional Stratum manufacturing decision policy

SM-PROT-07 is internal synthetic policy. It authorizes these bounded design decisions for this dataset:

- Keep the 430 by 250 mm footprint and 80 mm exterior tray height. If electronics clear-height wording conflicts, use a 75 mm top of the keepout and record the resolution in the design document.
- Proposed stock and finishes are preferences until current supplier capability is retrieved. Do not silently swap alloy, thickness, radius, or coating.
- Base and lid remain separate indexed CAD parts and separate developed DXFs. No flattened assembly drawing is an acceptable replacement.
- Use laser cutting and bending; no welding is planned. If corner construction or lid fasteners require additional processes, expose the change before CAD release.
- Manual and test-auto reviews must retain exact revision/digest identity. A test auto decision is not evidence of engineering correctness.
- Only a local simulated supplier preview is authorized. No real upload, cart change, purchase, payment, production release, or communication is authorized.
- Prototype reviews cannot claim compliance with an enclosure, rack, EMC, electrical, or safety standard. No such standard has been selected for this study.
