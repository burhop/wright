# User profile

Felix Arden is the fictional industrial designer at the fictional Stratum Measurement Studio. He works with a mechanical engineer and a purchasing lead on bench instruments. He supplies front-panel intent, electronics keepouts, finish goals, and company fabrication rules, and wants multiple sheet-metal parts kept as a coherent assembly. He values styling and service access but expects manufacturing limits to reshape the concept when necessary.

The people, business, product, schedule, and all design measurements are synthetic.
