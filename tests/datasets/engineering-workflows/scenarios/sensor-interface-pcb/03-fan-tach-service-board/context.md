# Fan tach service adapter brief
Original synthetic engineering inputs; no claimed certification.
## Business and system assumptions
Circlet makes portable air-analysis instruments. The service prototype uses a current-limited 24 V bench supply, maximum 0.8 A total, and a separate regulated 3.3 V logic reference. Each fan is assumed to draw 0.30 A continuous and 0.40 A start current. Fans have an open-collector tach output that tolerates a 3.3 V pull-up and shares power ground; this is a required compatibility assumption to verify against the selected fan datasheet. Do not connect a push-pull 24 V tach output.
J1 carries +24V/GND power in; J2/J3 each carry +24V/GND/TACH_A_RAW or TACH_B_RAW to a fan. J4 carries 3V3,TACH_A_OUT,TACH_B_OUT,GND to a diagnostic controller. R1/R2 are 10 kΩ pull-ups from 3V3 to the two raw tach nets. R3/R4 are 1 kΩ series resistors from raw tach to corresponding output. C1/C2 are 1 nF from each output to GND. TP1/TP2 are raw/output A; TP3/TP4 raw/output B; TP5 GND. Tach frequency design band 0..500 Hz; downstream digital input is assumed high impedance and 3.3 V compatible.
## Layout requirements and limitations
Use two-layer routing. Reserve at least 1.0 mm track width for each fan power/return branch and 1.5 mm for common supply/return trunks, treated as internal starting requirements to check thermally later. Keep 24 V and logic signal copper at least 0.8 mm apart except at defined ground connections. J1 is 1x02 3.5 mm-pitch screw terminal; J2/J3 are 1x03 2.54 mm prototype headers; J4 is 1x04 2.54 mm header. Headers are not approved production connectors. Place power entry near bottom edge and J4 at top. Mark 24V, GND, A/B and pin 1. This is neither a mains PCB nor a safety controller; do not infer isolation from layout spacing.
Use final geometric connector centers J3=(36.0,16.46) mm and J4=(20.0,25.5) mm. These coordinates are connector centers, not footprint origins. Place the TP3 pad center at (32.0,14.0) mm. These positions retain an inward routing corridor for the 1.0 mm copper-to-edge rule; preserve them through autorouting.
The retained-board route qualification also proved unconstrained placements J2 footprint origin=(5.0,19.0) mm and TP1 footprint origin=(10.0,14.0) mm. Use these exact origins when routing this revision; they preserve the J2 GND corridor and avoid the J2/TP1 courtyard overlap. They are footprint origins, not geometric connector or pad centers.
