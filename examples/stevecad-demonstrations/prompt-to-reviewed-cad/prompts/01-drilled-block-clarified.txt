Create one solid rectangular block, 60 mm along X, 40 mm along Y and 12 mm along Z. Use millimetres and the lower-left bottom corner as origin: the block occupies X=0..60, Y=0..40, Z=0..12.

Add four plain diameter-6 mm through holes parallel to Z, centered at XY (10,10), (50,10), (10,30) and (50,30). Each hole must traverse the full thickness. Do not add fillets, chamfers, counterbores, text or extra components.

Name the CAD body block and the four individual hole features h01, h02, h03 and h04. Use lowercase snake_case for CAD feature identifiers. For this non-centered block's block.top face, use the listed positive XY hole coordinates directly as the Model.hole point tuples, without subtracting the block's half dimensions. This coordinate convention was verified on the preceding block's actual geometry.

This first exercise verifies digital CAD geometry.
