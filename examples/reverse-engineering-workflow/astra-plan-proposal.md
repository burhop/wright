# Reverse-engineering workflow plan
Asset set: re-workflow/1.0.0. Status: proposed authoring assets; no tools, providers, validators, reconstructions or tests were executed. This document describes bindings to Wright's accepted .wflow source and existing compiler/runtime, not a replacement executor or a newly qualified capability.

## 1. Scope and claim contract
Declare the intended claim before selecting evidence or methods: appearance, envelope, dimensional reconstruction or functional replacement. These claims are not interchangeable. Appearance is limited to declared views and conditions; envelope requires bounds on relevant extents; dimensional reconstruction requires traceable dimensions and uncertainties; functional replacement additionally needs supplied functional requirements and qualified verification beyond geometry. Unknown hidden geometry prevents claims that depend on it. Keep five results separate: geometry fidelity, CAD intent/topology, exact export integrity, optional manufacturing checks and human acceptance. No physical actions, production release or automatic approvals are authorized.

## 2. Required run inputs
Bind these to Wright's existing declared typed-input mechanism before unattended execution: claim and exclusions; object revision/configuration and component scope; requirement authorities and conflict resolution; coordinate frame, handedness, axes, datums and units; requirement-specific tolerances and decision rules; authorized evidence and rights; reference and acceptance-profile identities; required producer, CAD kernel and export formats/versions; method qualification requirements; required independent audit coverage; correction parameter allowlist, bounds, coupling rules, correction budget and non-improvement rule; provider/model access; storage and execution bounds. Missing required choices produce input_required for dependent steps. Missing executable tools produce capability_unavailable for dependent steps. Do not manufacture defaults. Typed input completion is not review approval.

Configure storage per run: maximum retained bytes and artifacts, scratch bounds, request/response-log retention under existing redaction, evidence/artifact retention, cleanup permissions and checkpoint policy. If required provenance cannot fit, hold rather than silently evict originals, snapshots, exact export bytes or audit records. Preserve immutable external evidence through accessible hash-addressed references where authorized.

## 3. Evidence and requirement records
Use EngineeringResult/provenance and the common workspace view. Record many-to-many component/feature/view links; original URI and content hash; provenance and rights; object revision and configuration; photo pixel dimensions; video timestamp, timebase and shot; camera calibration and distortion, crop and resize transforms; visible and occluded regions; scale-anchor identities and uncertainty; capture and publication groups; and fitting, development or audit role. Preserve original bytes and lineage for derivatives. Repeated frames, shared crops and republications are correlated evidence, not independent views. Keep different generations and object states partitioned; any authorized cross-state inference remains explicitly conditional.

Each requirement records authority, scope, frame/datum, units, tolerance, decision rule, classification (measured, published, calculated, inferred, assumed or unknown), evidence/input IDs and uncertainty. Calculated values include formulas, input revisions and correlations. Propagate uncertainty using a declared applicable method; unavailable covariance or unresolved bias must be reported rather than treated as zero. Assumed values require explicit authorization and labels. Unknowns never silently become CAD dimensions. A hypothesis may use an explicitly authorized parameter range without converting that range into a verified dimension.

## 4. Method and independence rules
Select calibrated 3D-to-2D projection for view comparisons, silhouettes and landmarks for supported visible features, drawing sections/dimensions for applicable documented geometry, and bidirectional surface comparison where an independent 3D reference exists. Visual hull is conditional on adequate calibrated views/masks and cannot recover concavities merely by intersecting silhouettes. SfM/MVS requires suitable texture, overlap, static state, calibration and observable scale; its output remains subject to uncertainty and independent checks. Learned geometry or generative image-to-mesh is an uncertain hypothesis source, never a hidden dimensional authority. Gloss, glass, refraction, transparency, occlusion, scale ambiguity and depth ambiguity can invalidate observations or enlarge uncertainty. Silhouettes alone cannot verify hidden depth.

Partition evidence by independent source groups before fitting. Audit cameras, masks, landmarks and registration rules are selected independently of the candidate and frozen before candidate scoring. A crop from a fitting photograph cannot become an independent audit image. Shared calibration, scale anchors or publication ancestry must be disclosed as residual dependence. Audit information must not guide correction; once disclosed for fitting, retire its holdout role and require fresh independent audit evidence or report the limitation.

## 5. Milestones and exit criteria
M0 — Local authoring assets: preserve this plan and the three versioned instruction assets through Wright's saving/snapshot mechanism. Translate logical bindings into accepted .wflow using existing supported syntax. Exit: compiler acceptance, declared typed inputs and review boundaries, identifiable instruction snapshots and dependency graph. Compilation alone does not qualify execution.
M1 — Deterministic fixture qualification: use pre-existing synthetic geometry and its existing authoritative requirements, fixed fixture cameras and known dimensions. Do not generate replacement fixtures to make tests pass. Exercise evidence indexing, projection/measurement, revision validation and the four mandatory negative controls: wrong scale, changed hash, wrong camera and missing depth. Exit: every applicable fixture assertion produces its specified outcome, repeated runs agree under the fixture's declared numeric rules, and failure evidence is retained. If no suitable fixture or executable measurement implementation exists, hold this milestone. This is neither real CAD reconstruction nor live-provider qualification.
M2 — Requirements and observability: index authorized project evidence only when supplied; resolve claim-critical authority conflicts and classify supported, conditional and unobservable requirements. Exit: every claim requirement has a qualified verification path or an explicit unresolved disposition; required missing inputs are declared before unattended operation. Unobservable requirements cannot receive a pass.
M3 — Capability qualification and freeze: qualify actual providers/tools for the intended operations, file formats and method scope. Verify model-request enforcement and capture on the actual request path. Freeze evidence hashes, source partitions, calibration/crop/distortion, cameras, masks, landmarks, frame/datums, scale, requirement/profile/reference identities, tool configuration and instruction snapshots. Exit: all mandatory dependencies are qualified for their stated scope and the immutable run manifest is bound to the revision. Synthetic success is not sufficient evidence of real-photo accuracy.
M4 — Editable CAD production: invoke only a qualified producer with the frozen contract. Exit: native editable CAD is retrievable with parameter/feature mapping and recorded topology/intent checks; exact exports have hashes, units, frame and format metadata. If the available producer emits only a mesh, do not label it editable parametric CAD.
M5 — Exact-output measurement: measure the exact candidate revision, native artifact where relevant and exact export bytes separately. Exit: each applicable requirement has a traceable result with uncertainty, coverage, method and disposition; measurement failures and unknowns are explicit, not omitted. No universal tolerance is introduced.
M6 — Bounded correction: change only named authorized parameters, using development measurements rather than audit holdouts. Exit: declared criteria are met or a configured stop is reached; every attempted revision, delta and result is retained. Passing some requirements does not authorize regression of protected requirements.
M7 — Independent audit and report: audit exact frozen artifacts using an independently qualified verifier and reserved evidence. Exit: audit results are revision-bound and the report separates geometry, CAD intent/topology, export integrity, optional manufacturing results, uncertainties, unsupported claims and pending human acceptance. A failed audit does not silently restart fitting against the holdout.
M8 — Declared human review: Wright handles review against the exact revision and report. Exit, if subsequently performed: an explicit review disposition bound to that revision. This authoring response neither performs nor predicts review.

## 6. Runtime and invalidation
Freeze model gpt-6-astra and reasoning setting high. Before every observed model request, including helpers, correction and recovery, reject configuration drift or unavailable support. Capture actual observable requests/responses and available provider metadata through existing redaction and provenance. Do not claim visibility into provider internals or infer hidden execution settings from model prose. No silent fallback. If the existing path cannot enforce or observe required settings, hold model-dependent steps pending integration.

Use durable runtime mutation identity, checkpoints and revision-bound validator/rework. Never replay a mutation with uncertain outcome: reconcile recorded operation identity and resulting artifacts through existing mechanisms; if certainty cannot be restored, hold. A completed operation is not retried merely because a later dependency is missing.

Invalidate descendants explicitly: evidence/hash/state/rights changes invalidate affected evidence use and dependent fits/results; calibration/camera/crop/mask/frame/scale changes invalidate affected measurements and geometry derived from them; requirements, authority, tolerance, profile or reference changes invalidate dependent decisions; instruction/model/tool changes invalidate the affected qualification scope and derived results. CAD edits invalidate all geometry-dependent exports, measurements and audit for that revision; re-exporting invalidates export-bound results even when native CAD is unchanged. Audit leakage invalidates holdout independence. Never transfer approval to a new revision automatically.

## 7. Portability and reuse
Portable assets are source instructions, supported .wflow source, immutable snapshots, manifest contracts, requirement/evidence identities, fixtures when licensed and result/provenance references. Exclude credentials, provider sessions, machine paths, transient caches, proprietary kernels/licenses, unavailable source evidence, nonredistributable artifacts and undeclared vendor guarantees. A target workspace must re-resolve access and requalify environment-dependent capabilities; portable snapshots do not make providers portable.

A substantially different existing time-series robot-tracking example may exercise generic evidence lineage, timestamps, state separation, durable execution, request capture and revision invalidation under its original requirements. It does not qualify CAD production, projection accuracy or reconstruction. A later sheet-metal example can qualify drawing/section methods under that example's original requirements and supplied manufacturing rules; do not invent bend allowances, thicknesses or tolerances.

## 8. Ownership and completion boundary
Wright already owns source compilation, durable scheduling, gateway/MCP invocation, EngineeringResult/provenance, revision-bound validation/rework, typed-input/review handling, instruction snapshots and workspace presentation. The proposed work is to bind engineering-specific contracts, qualified producers/verifiers, request guards and checks into those facilities where absent. No new executor, hidden policy literals, vendor workaround or UI redesign is proposed. Saved plans, schemas and unexecuted tests are authoring artifacts only. Capability and run statuses must distinguish authored, available, integration-needed, qualified-for-scope and blocked; only evidence of actual execution supports a qualification claim.
