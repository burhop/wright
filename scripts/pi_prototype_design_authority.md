# Prototype design choices and source facts

The workflow is being asked to design a real proposed prototype from a human
brief. Unspecified design choices are part of that design work. Choose and record
shell seam height, fillets, foot dimensions, light-viewing opening, screw length,
access clearance, boss layout and other custom part dimensions within the supplied
envelope, style, mounting and service requirements. Label these as proposed
designer-selected dimensions, with fit checks outstanding. They are not measured
customer data or manufacturer facts.

Read the actual primary drawing pixels to map dimension leaders to board features.
Record the source URL, raw source hash, page/view and reference-only limitation.
Never recover missing manufacturer measurements from the styling sketch or invent
a component rating. If a connector body has no sourced full envelope, a removable
open side panel or deliberately generous custom access opening can be a documented
prototype design choice. Preserve all required access and service functions and
record the physical-fit uncertainty; do not silently omit the function.

The reference operation makes a candidate ruthex RX-M2.5x5.7 supplier table and
installation illustration available. Inspect them before selecting that insert
and creating its pocket/boss. Use the actual table row and its diagram labels;
do not treat a product image without dimensions as a dimensional specification.
Choose the surrounding custom plastic geometry as an explicit prototype proposal,
respecting the sourced minimums. Any missing installation process, torque, pull-out,
durability or material qualification stays unverified. Those checks are not
authorization to purchase, manufacture or release a production drawing.

Use passive boundary conditions for the two passive briefs. Only the fan brief
uses its uploaded synthetic curve. Do not turn a generic solver capability into
an extra customer requirement. Keep original heat loads, source locations, actual
two-variant CAD and matching fluid domains; never substitute prerequisite geometry.

The local design review accepts the documented proposal for test execution.
It does not supply missing source facts or certify physical/thermal correctness.
Retain every genuine unresolved requirement in the design document.
