# Representative engineer usability protocol

## Participants

Recruit at least five practicing mechanical/manufacturing/design engineers who were not involved in building the recovery concept. Record role and relevant workflow-tool experience without collecting unnecessary personal or customer data. Obtain the appropriate consent before recording notes, screen, audio, or accessibility output.

## Moderator rules

- Explain that the surface is provisional and simulated; it cannot execute, publish, or approve engineering work.
- Ask the participant to think aloud. Do not teach the port treatment, component controls, or expected path before the task.
- Use the exact task wording below. Record help only after the participant becomes blocked, and mark that task assisted.
- Preserve errors, hesitation, misclicks, inaccessible controls, and unexpected interpretation verbatim where consent permits.
- Stop on a safety/authority misunderstanding, a material product-direction concern, or an inaccessible required action.

## Tasks

1. Identify what the bracket workflow consumes and produces, then distinguish a connection socket from the artifact inspection action.
2. Add the tolerance inspection block, connect it correctly, undo it, and restore it.
3. Expand the reusable design-review component and identify the exact internal concept targeted by a supplied diagnostic/run record.
4. Switch to Split, change one valid configuration value in source, apply it, then recover from one invalid source edit without losing the accepted graph.
5. Review an AI multi-block proposal, explain what acceptance does and does not authorize, reject once, then accept a fresh reviewed proposal.
6. Start the simulated run, diagnose the missing material, recover with 6061-T6, and identify output lineage without mistaking the fixture for generated engineering evidence.
7. Repeat representative selection, component disclosure, connection, modal, and source-view actions using keyboard only. If applicable, repeat with the participant's screen reader at 200% browser zoom.

## Measures and gate

For every task record completion, assistance, time, wrong target/actions, reversals, authority misunderstandings, accessibility barriers, and participant confidence. Do not average away a safety or authority error.

The gate passes only if every participant can complete the safety-critical authority and artifact/handle distinctions without moderator correction, no required action is inaccessible, and no unresolved material product-direction ambiguity remains. Other errors require documented severity, repair, and rerun criteria. A numerical success rate alone cannot close the gate.

## Required evidence

- Exact subject identities and environment for each session.
- Consent/privacy disposition.
- Timestamped task-level notes and observed outcomes.
- Any recordings or screenshots referenced by digest and access policy.
- Consolidated findings with severity and disposition.
- Repair subject and rerun evidence for every gate-blocking finding.
- Explicit product decision; never infer approval from attendance or task completion.
