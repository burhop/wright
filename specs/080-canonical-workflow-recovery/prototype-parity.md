## What is deliberately not copied

- Prototype-owned semantic or run authority.
- Automatic AI mutation or execution affordances.
- Vendor graph serialization as persistence.
- The frozen shell’s lane/form-first grammar.
- Claims from the prototype’s unrun five-person study.

## Current conclusion

The recovery concept reaches functional parity with the useful visual direction and surpasses the frozen prototype on direct manipulation, paired editing, proposal review, invalid containment, and bounded run recovery. It does not establish production persistence/runtime integration, representative usability, or qualifying scale. Those remain downstream of product approval.
